Practical guidance on AI in higher education.

Helping you engage with the challenges and opportunities that AI presents.

Almost every student now uses generative AI: 95% of UK undergraduates do, yet fewer than half feel supported by their teaching staff (HEPI, 2026). Closing that gap is mostly a matter of design. In assessment, that means tasks that still show learning and a clear account of authorship. In the classroom, it means activities where AI builds the material and students do the judging, AI literacy built into each discipline, and teaching students to read and write critically alongside it. And it opens real possibilities, from feedback that is abundant while judgement stays human to learning tools educators can now build themselves.
