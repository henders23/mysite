Before you book

  • Who is it for?

    AI-Ready Educator is for teams that want to use AI confidently and critically. Vibe Coding for Educational Purposes is for teams that want to build their own interactive learning tools, and needs no coding experience.

    The Module & Assessment Redesign Lab is for one programme team that needs redesigned assessment ready for an exam board. Consultancy is for strategy, policy or staff development at faculty or institution scale. The AI Collegium is for individual educators who want a cohort to think with.

  • Online or in person?

    The workshops and the Lab are delivered online to your whole team, and the Collegium is an online cohort. Consultancy is designed around your context.

  • When do sessions run?

    The workshops and the Lab run on Fridays. Workshop sessions are 9.30–12.30 UK time, and the two half-days are a few weeks apart, so colleagues can try ideas with students in between and come back with real observations. The Lab runs three half-day sessions across six weeks, with drafting in between. The Collegium runs from January to June 2027.

    Other days or times: [Paul to confirm].

  • How many staff can take part?

    Up to 20 staff for each workshop and up to 12 for the Lab. Workshops and the Lab are charged per team, not per person.

    For several departments or a whole institution, consultancy covers multi-department and institution-wide capability programmes.

  • What do you need from us?

    For AI-Ready Educator, bring your own modules and assessment briefs: the sessions work on them. For Vibe Coding, colleagues build tools for their own modules between sessions. For the Lab, bring one module or programme. The team drafts revised briefs between sessions, and I give written feedback on each draft.

  • Can it be tailored to our discipline?

    Yes. The workshops are delivered for your team using your own modules and assessment briefs. The Lab works on your module or programme. Consultancy is designed around your context rather than a fixed format.

  • What will staff come away with?

    AI-Ready Educator: a shared position on AI across the team, a practical design framework, and at least one usable artefact or process applied to your own modules.

    Vibe Coding: working tools the team built themselves, published with a link or embedded in your VLE, and a repeatable workflow for making more.

    The Lab: redesigned assessment briefs for the module, marking criteria aligned to them, a student-facing AI-use statement, and short guidance for whoever teaches it next. The Lab fee covers redesign of up to six assessment briefs, with one round of written feedback per draft.

  • How is impact measured?

    The workshops are built to show change in practice: colleagues try ideas with students between the two sessions and bring back what happened. The Lab ends by pressure-testing the package against what an exam board and an integrity panel will ask.

  • Will our own staff be able to carry it on?

    That is the aim at scale. Consultancy staff development programmes are built so that your own educational developers can sustain them after I step back.
